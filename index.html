The Sacred Bond

Nikah — نِكَاح

The Nikah is Islam's marriage covenant — a solemn contract between two souls witnessed before Allah and the community. It is half of one's deen, the Sunnah of the Prophet ﷺ, and a celebration of love, mercy and tranquillity.

Wali (Guardian)

The bride's guardian — her father or nearest male relative — gives her in marriage with her free and full consent.

Witnesses (Shuhud)

Two adult Muslim male witnesses of sound character must be present for the marriage contract to be valid.

Ijab wa Qabul

The offer (Ijab) from the bride's side and acceptance (Qabul) from the groom — the verbal exchange that binds the union.

Mahr (Dowry)

A mandatory gift — financial or otherwise — given by the groom to the bride as a symbol of honour and commitment.

Requirements of Marriage

The Five Pillars of Nikah

01

Proposal

The formal offer of marriage from the groom or his representative.

02

Acceptance

The groom's clear verbal acceptance in the same sitting.

03

Wali

The bride's guardian acting on her behalf with her full consent.

04

Two Witnesses

Two upright Muslim male witnesses present at the contract.

05

Mahr

An agreed gift from groom to bride, paid promptly or deferred.

The Day's Programme

Order of Ceremony

11:00 AM

Arrival & Welcome

Guests are welcomed and seated. The hall is perfumed with oud and rose water as is the Sunnah.

11:30 AM

Recitation of Qur'an

The ceremony opens with Surah Al-Fatiha and selected ayat seeking Allah's blessings.

11:45 AM

Khutbah al-Nikah

The Imam delivers the marriage sermon — rights and responsibilities of spouses in Islam.

12:00 PM

Ijab wa Qabul

The offer and acceptance — the heart of the Nikah — witnessed by family and all present.

12:20 PM

Signing of the Aqd

The marriage contract is signed by the bride, groom, wali, witnesses, and officiating Imam.

12:40 PM

Du'a for the Couple

The Imam leads the congregation in supplication — Barakallahu lakuma wa baraka alaykuma.

1:30 PM

Walimah — Wedding Feast

The celebration feast begins. Sharing food with guests is a blessed Sunnah of the Prophet ﷺ.
